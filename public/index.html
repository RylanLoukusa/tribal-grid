A daily Survivor trivia grid. Name a castaway who fits each row and column. The fewer players who think of your pick, the better you score. New grid every day at midnight Eastern. How to play · Past answers

  

    How to play
    
The short version is below. The full guide has a scoring example and tips, and the clue guide explains every clue.

    
    
      
  • Pick a square and name a castaway who fits both its row and its column. Every US season that has finished airing counts.
    • 
      
  • Right answers are free, and you can swap a right answer for a rarer one any time before you submit. You get 9 misses in total.
    • 
      
  • Each right answer scores the percent of players who picked the same person, so a pick 3% of players made adds 3 points. Your score is those percents added up, with 100 for each empty square. Low wins: a perfect grid of rare picks scores under 50, and a blank grid scores 900.
    • 
      
  • When you submit, tap any square to see every right answer, rarest first.
    • 
      
  • Right answers are colour-coded by how few players picked them:
        🟪 Legendary, under 1%🟦 Rare, 1–5%🟩 Uncommon, 5–15%🟨 Common, 15%+⬛ Empty
    • 
      
  • A new grid comes out every day at midnight Eastern.
    • 
    

  

  

    About
    
Castaway facts come from survivoR, an open dataset by Dan Oehm (MIT licence). "Listed as from" uses the hometown shown on any of their seasons. Seasons still airing aren't counted, so nothing here spoils an episode.

    
Rarity starts from how well known each castaway is and shifts to real picks as people submit. Think an answer was marked wrong? Submit the grid, tap that square and use "Report a problem".
